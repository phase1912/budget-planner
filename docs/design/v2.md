# Design v2 — the reference since F23

The look the product moved to after the App Store comparison (E23, ADR-0022). The screens
are in `screens/v2/`, rendered from the design canvas
(https://claude.ai/artifact/J4TvghztEyxSjmfyNWXqet) at a phone's 390 px; this file gives
the numbers they are built from, so "it does not look like the canvas" is checkable.

Where a v2 screen and an older one in `screens/` draw the same thing, **v2 wins**. The old
screens stay for what v2 does not draw: the upload wizard's conflict steps, categories,
the receipts list and the receipt dialog, the public pages, login and register. Those take
v2's type, radii and surfaces without a screen of their own.

## Principles

1. **One number first.** Every tab opens on the answer — what is left, what was spent,
   which price moved — in a ring or a big figure, with the detail beneath.
2. **Colour carries meaning, words do not repeat it.** A category is its colour and its
   picture everywhere: tiles, rings, rows, bars.
3. **Fewer words.** A title, one line of help at most. Explanations live in the BRD and
   in Help, not under every control.
4. **Tap to see more.** Rings change view, rows open, a "some items" link reveals the
   choice — the complex path stays one tap away instead of on screen.
5. **Function is kept.** v2 re-lays every existing capability; nothing is dropped to
   match a picture.

## Type

Manrope, 400–800, served with the app (`@fontsource-variable/manrope`, never from another
origin — `lint:privacy`). The canvas was drawn in Plus Jakarta Sans, which has no basic
Cyrillic, so Ukrainian would have fallen back to another face; Manrope has the same
geometric build and covers all three languages. Numbers are `tabular-nums`.

| Role | Size / weight | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Screen title | 28 / 800, −0.02em | "Spending", "Products", "Plan", "Settings" |
| Month on Home | 24 / 800 | between the arrows |
| Hero figure | 24–32 / 800 | ring centres, "You paid" |
| Section title | 18 / 800 | "Where it went", "Recent", "Goals" |
| Card title | 17 / 800 | inside a card's head |
| Row title | 15 / 700 | list rows, tiles' amounts at 18 / 800 |
| Body | 15–16 / 400–600 | onboarding text, hints |
| Secondary | 13 / 500–600, muted | dates, shops, "of which", meta |
| Section label | 13 / 700, uppercase, +0.06em, muted | "PALETTE", "MONEY" in Settings |
| Tab label | 11 / 700 | bottom bar |

Nothing reads below 12 px.

## Geometry

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--radius-hero` | 28 px | the hero card (Home's ring, Spending's ring) |
| `--radius-card` | 22 px | every card, tile and list card |
| `--radius-control` | 16 px | inputs, segmented control, row buttons |
| `--radius-chip` | 12 px | segmented items, icon tiles (40 px) |
| `--radius-pill` | 9999 px | pills, rings, avatars |
| Primary button | 56 px high, radius 18, 17 / 800 | one per screen at most |
| Touch target | ≥ 44 px | every control |

Spacing: 20 px screen gutter on a phone, 18 px between sections, 16–20 px card padding,
10–12 px between tiles.

## Surfaces

The page is the palette's ground (`--color-muted`); cards are `--color-surface`, flat,
no border, a soft shadow at most. Lists are **one** card with 1 px dividers, not a card
per row. A control (field, secondary button, filter) has no border either: it is filled
with the colour of what it is not on — the card colour on the ground, the ground inside a
card, sheet or dialog. `.control-fill` does this, and `Card` and `Modal` mark themselves
`.on-surface` so the swap is automatic (`frontend/src/shared/styles/index.css`); a focused
field shows a 2 px primary border. A hint or a "to review" strip is a tinted card (`--tone-primary-bg`). Midnight's
ground is darker than its cards so cards read.

## Components

- **Navigation.** Phone: a bar of Home · Spending · ＋ · Products · Plan, the ＋ a 56 px
  primary square (radius 18) lifted 22 px above the bar; no header above the screens —
  each screen has its own title, and Home's avatar opens Settings. Tablet: the same
  places as an icon rail; desktop: a side menu with names, the brand on top and the
  account at the bottom.
- **Ring** (`Ring`). Conic gradient of palette tokens, band 16 % of the radius on Home,
  30 % on Spending; figure, currency and a label in the middle. Tapping changes the view;
  dots beneath say which.
- **Fact rows.** Label left, value right, 15 / 700 on 56 px rows; the editable ones
  (income, budget) open an amount dialog.
- **Category tile.** 40 px icon tile tinted 15 % of the category colour, name 13 muted,
  amount 18 / 800, a 6 px bar in the category colour; two across on a phone, four on a
  desktop.
- **Segmented control.** A 16 px-radius track in the card colour, items 12 px radius, the
  chosen one filled primary with the primary foreground.
- **Settings rows.** Grouped under an uppercase label, each row a 36 px icon tile, a title
  and a value or a switch, a chevron when it opens something.
- **Sheets and dialogs.** A bottom sheet on a phone (radius 28 on top), a centred dialog
  on wider screens — `Modal` does both, so every dialog follows.
- **Back.** A screen opened from another tab (Goals and Trips from Plan, a trip from its
  list) carries a 44 px round back button above its title (`BackLink`); a holiday goes
  back to Plan, a business trip to its list.
- **Section switch.** "Receipts | Categories" sits on top of both screens at every width:
  neither is a tab, so the switch is how one reaches the other.
- **Onboarding.** Full screen, no navigation; step dots, Back and Skip on top, one primary
  button at the bottom. A palette picked there paints the app at once.

## What the canvas draws that the product does differently

- The upload start draws a live viewfinder; the web opens the phone's own camera instead
  (a viewfinder waits for the Android app, E22).
- Products' "Price changes" sparkline uses the last six prices bought; "Cheapest shop"
  counts the shop cheapest for most products, from the user's own receipts only.
- Onboarding's income step draws one big amount; the product also asks for payday and
  what is on the accounts today (the balance's start, F23.14) on the same step.
- On a tablet and desktop, Plan lays its cards in two columns and Products, Spending and
  Home widen their grids; the canvas only draws the phone.
- Spending's "By day" bars show for periods up to about two months; longer ones keep the
  ring and the table.
- The goal check's sources are the categories most is spent on, each trimmed by 15 %;
  the canvas's shop-switch idea ("Lidl for the weekly basket") is not suggested yet.
- Home also carries two prompts the canvas does not draw: "Has your salary arrived?" on
  payday, and a balance at zero asking for missing income (domain model 30).
