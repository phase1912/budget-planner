# Play policy checklist — the Android app (F22.4.1)

Each row was checked against the Play policy page linked from it, on the date in the last
column. Re-read the pages and update the rows before every release to production.

| Policy | What it asks | The app's answer | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Target API level](https://developer.android.com/google/play/requirements/target-sdk) (page updated 2026-10-16) | From 31 August 2026, new apps and updates target Android 16 (API 36); an extension can be asked for until 1 November 2026 | `targetSdkVersion = 36`, `compileSdkVersion = 36`, `minSdkVersion = 24` (`frontend/android/variables.gradle`) | 2026-10-23 |
| [Permissions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9888170) | Ask only for what a feature needs and say why | `INTERNET`; `CAMERA` for receipt photos; `RECORD_AUDIO` and `MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS` for a voice message to the assistant, only while recording; `VIBRATE` (haptics plugin). No storage or media permissions are declared. **Open:** gallery images through the system photo picker and the camera-denied fallback (F22.4.3, #1194). | 2026-10-23 |
| [Data safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469) | A form matching what the app collects and shares | From ADR-0020, the answers are:<ul><li>**Collected:** name and email (account), financial info (incomes, balances, purchase history from receipts), photos, other user content (receipts, conversations), app interactions (F17.3.1).</li><li>**Shared:** nothing, since Google Cloud and Vertex AI are processors.</li><li>**Encryption:** in transit.</li><li>**Deletion:** users can ask for their data to be deleted.</li></ul> | 2026-10-23 |
| [Account deletion](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111) | Deleting the account in the app, and a web link to ask for it without the app | <ul><li>**In the app:** Settings › Your data › Delete account, with the password (F17.9.1).</li><li>**On the web:** `https://budget-agent.web.app/settings`, after signing in. The Help page explains it (`help.json`, "Delete account").</li></ul> | 2026-10-23 |
| [Financial features declaration](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13849271) | Declare any financial feature | Personal budgeting and expense tracking only: no loans, no payments, no banking, no crypto, no investing advice | 2026-10-23 |
| [AI-generated content](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14094294) | A generative AI app must not generate offensive content; users report it in the app | The assistant is a text chatbot central to the app.<ul><li>Every reply can be reported (F22.4.2).</li><li>The model runs with Vertex AI's default safety filters.</li></ul> | 2026-10-23 |
| Content rating (IARC questionnaire) | Answer the questionnaire honestly | No violence, sexual content, gambling or user-to-user chat. Members of a household see shared budget figures only. Expected: Everyone / PEGI 3. | 2026-10-23 |
| [Families](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9893335) | Target audience and ads | Target audience 18+ (adults managing a household budget). Not designed for children; no ads in the app yet. In-app ads (E19) and a child role would reopen this row. | 2026-10-23 |
| [Metadata](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898842) | No misleading claims, no keyword stuffing, screenshots of the real app | <ul><li>Name "Budget Agent".</li><li>Screenshots from the demo account.</li><li>No country or currency in the copy.</li><li>No claims about savings figures.</li></ul> | 2026-10-23 |
